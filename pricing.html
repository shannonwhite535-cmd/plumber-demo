Honest pricing

Real ranges. No "from $0" nonsense.

Most plumbers will not put a number on the page. I will. These are the actual ranges for the jobs I do most often. Final price after I have seen it.

Why ranges, not fixed prices?

Every house is different. A tap washer replacement on a modern mixer is half the time of one on a 1970s wall-mounted tap with a damaged seat. Honest ranges respect that, while still telling you what you are in for. The bottom of the range is "easy job, no surprises". The top is "the tap is older than your grandkids".
