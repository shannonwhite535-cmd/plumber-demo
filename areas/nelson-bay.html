Plumbing in Nelson Bay

Nelson Bay is a coastal town with a mix of permanent residences, holiday homes and short-term rental properties. Housing ranges from 1960s beach shacks to brand-new architect-designed waterfront homes.

Saltwater air is the dominant factor in Nelson Bay plumbing. Brass and copper external fittings corrode faster here than anywhere else in the Hunter. Outdoor taps, garden plumbing, hot water unit external fittings and metalwork on solar systems all need closer monitoring.

Holiday rentals and Airbnb properties have specific compliance needs. Smoke alarms get attention, but plumbing and gas don’t. If your rental has a gas appliance, the two-yearly servicing rule applies and I can do the servicing and certificate together. Don’t want to be the landlord who finds out the wrong way.
